# Part 2: Design

### 1. What overall style will best represent you (minimalist, creative, professional, etc.)?

My website will have a clean layout with playful elements. I want to reflect my personality while keeping the content organized and easy to navigate.

### 2. What color scheme will you use and why?

I will be using a pink color scheme with pink backgrounds, lighter pink and white sections, and a darker pink for buttons and links. I will use a darker color for text to provide contrast and improve readability. I'm going with this color scheme because pink is my favorite color.

### 3. What fonts will you use for headings and body text?

I plan to use Poppins for headings and Arial for body text. Poppins has a modern, rounded appearance that fits the playful design of my website, while Arial is simple and easy to read.

### 4. How will your design reflect your personality or field?

My design will reflect my personality through the color scheme and decorative details. The layout will be clean and organized to reflect my interest in technology and design.

### 5. What layout will your homepage follow?

My homepage will have a navigation bar at the top, followed by a welcome section introducing myself and buttons linking to my Projects and About Me pages. Below, there will be a section displaying my technical skills. The bottom of the homepage will have a contact section.

### 6. How will you organize project sections visually?

My Projects page will use a grid of rounded project cards. Each card will have a project screenshot, title, short description, technologies used, and a button to view more information.

### 7. Will the site be mobile-friendly? How will you ensure responsiveness?

Yes, I will use CSS Flexbox, Grid, media queries, and flexible sizing to adjust the layout for different screen sizes.

### 8. What visual hierarchy will guide visitors?

I will use larger headings, contrasting colors, and clearly styled buttons to draw attention to important information.

### 9. How will consistency be maintained across pages?

All pages will use the same color palette, fonts, button styles, and rounded design elements.

### 10. How will accessibility be considered (contrast, font size, readability)?

I will use readable font sizes, sufficient color contrast, descriptive alternative text for images, and clear navigation labels. I will also make sure that visitors can navigate the website using a keyboard.

### 11. Will you use icons, images, or illustrations? Why?

Yes. I will include a personal photo on my About Me page, screenshots of my projects, and icons representing different technical skills and tools. I may also include small decorative illustrations to add personality to the website.

### 12. What portfolio websites inspired your design?

My design was inspired by personal portfolio websites that combine minimal layouts with soft colors and illustrations. I wanted to create something that feels creative and playful while still being professional and easy to navigate.

# Part 3: Interactivity

### 1. What interactive elements will your site include (navigation menus, buttons, forms)?

My website will include a navigation menu, buttons linking to different pages, clickable project cards, and links to my professional profiles.

### 2. Will your site include a contact form? How will it work?

No, I will not be including a contact form. My Contact page will include my email address and a link to my LinkedIn profile so visitors can contact me directly.

### 3. What JavaScript features will you implement?

I plan to use JavaScript to create a mobile navigation menu that opens and closes when a visitor clicks the menu button.

### 4. How will users receive feedback from interactions?

Buttons and navigation links will change appearance when hovered over or selected. The mobile navigation menu will also visibly open and close when clicked.

### 5. How does interactivity improve the user experience?

Interactivity will make my website easier to navigate and allow visitors to explore my work without becoming overwhelmed by information. The navigation menu will make the website easier to use on different devices.
